# Daily work report — 9 October 2026

Project: Queen Mira International School ERP  
Area: HR Department (Documentation v0.1)  
Status: HR rules are in the API and the staff screen. Module tests passed.

## Done today

1. Checked the HR Department document against the screens and the API.
2. Enforced the confirmed rule that there is one HR role. HR Head and HR Executive are the same HR role.
3. Staff are created only by HR. Saving the profile creates the login and issues a temporary password. The person must change that password on first login.
4. A saved profile cannot be rewritten. Later changes are a department or role transfer, an increment, or an exit. The previous assignment stays in history.
5. Visibility follows the document. HR and MD see all staff. A department head sees their department. A staff member sees only their own profile.
6. Approved leave updates attendance to Leave. A permission request updates attendance to Permission. Leave balance is the yearly quota minus approved days.
7. An applied increment updates designation and salary. A paid payroll month, an applied increment, and an approved disciplinary record cannot be overwritten.
8. A completed exit deactivates the login and keeps the history.
9. Incentives are stored with the person, the reason, the amount, and the approver.
10. Fixed the employee form so the staff list page compiles again. Adding a person shows the full profile. Transferring a person shows only department, role, and reporting head.
